Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

CooptaIQ is preparing to launch. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through this website.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your full name, work email, company name and role if you provide them. We also record whether you opted into product updates.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to waitlist requests, share product updates if you opted in, and understand interest in CooptaIQ. We do not sell your information.

Forms

Waitlist submissions are processed by Formspree, which delivers them to our team and stores them in the Formspree dashboard. If you opt into product updates, your name and email may also be added to our Brevo newsletter list. Formspree's and Brevo's own privacy policies apply to their processing.

Cookies and local storage

We use essential cookies and local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference and your cookie choice. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from product updates at any time. To request access, correction or deletion of your waitlist information, contact the CooptaIQ team through the waitlist form and note that it is a privacy request.

Changes

We may update this policy as CooptaIQ develops. The latest version will remain on this page.
